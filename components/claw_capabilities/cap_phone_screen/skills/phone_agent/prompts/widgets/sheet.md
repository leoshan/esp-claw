Sheets (an editor over the screen, Cancel/✕ and Save/Done/✓ at the top):
- Save is irreversible: tap! it, after checking every field holds the wanted value. Cancel/✕
  discards the changes.
- After a save the sheet closes and the same corner holds another button of the screen below
  (e.g. the "+" of a list). Never tap that corner again to "save": look for the saved result and
  reply done when it is there.
