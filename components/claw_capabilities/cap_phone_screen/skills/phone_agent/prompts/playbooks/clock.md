match: clock, alarm, timer, stopwatch, 时钟, 闹钟, 计时器, 秒表
contexts: picker, sheet
Clock app (时钟):
- Alarm tab (闹钟): "+" at the top right opens the 添加闹钟 (Add Alarm) sheet; 编辑 (Edit) just left
  of it enters edit mode, where alarms can be deleted: avoid it unless the goal asks.
- In 添加闹钟 the time picker is at the top, ✓ (save) at the top right, ✕ (cancel) at the top left.
- A saved alarm shows up in the alarm list, switched on: check its time and 凌晨/早上/下午 word
  there, then reply done.
- Do not change or delete existing alarms unless the goal asks.
